On the whiteboard
The host

One whiteboard. No script.

Hitesh MalviyaOG · Crypto

Post Human Society started as a whiteboard and a live camera — an ongoing, unscripted attempt to think through what happens to money, work and identity once intelligence stops being scarce.

The whiteboard belongs to Hitesh Malviya, an OG in the crypto space and the founder behind DYOR, one of the best-known on-chain data and analytics tools in the industry. Here, he steps away from the dashboards to just think out loud — session by session, in front of whoever shows up to watch it happen.
